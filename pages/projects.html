Soteria

Soteria is a native app for small business management and logistics, it's currently in development.
The app handles inventory, services, labour, and it comes with a web portal for managing appointment bookings.

It's built with Tauri and Svelte on top of SQLite, using Rust, TypeScript and JavaScript. I'm managing it with a team using Agile and Scrum, with issues and tickets tracked in Jira.

Upcoming features: predicting inventory usage to make reordering more efficient, employee scheduling, and automating workload management.

Cardex

Cardex is a forum for car enthusiasts. Alongside the message boards, it has a car collection and configuration system that lets people share their projects, passions and interests.

It was a team project, organised with a Kanban board and GitHub issues. It runs on Node.js and Express with a React front end, and uses two databases: MongoDB on the NoSQL side and Oracle for SQL.
